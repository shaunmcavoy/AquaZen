What electrical does a hot tub need in BC?

Series One runs on a dedicated 15 amp 120V GFCI-protected outdoor receptacle, or a 40 amp 240V line. Series Two and Three need a dedicated 60 amp 240V line with a GFCI disconnect, installed by a licensed electrician under a permit. In most of BC that permit comes from Technical Safety BC, and some cities, including Burnaby, Vancouver and Surrey, issue their own.

Who should wire my hot tub?

Our electrical partner is Gerry Jansen. He knows our tubs better than anybody and wires them properly the first time. Call or text him at 778-229-2560.

Should the electrical be done before or after delivery?

Either works. We recommend after: once the tub is in place, your electrician comes once, sees exactly where the equipment is, and wires it start to finish in one visit.

What base does a hot tub need?

A solid, flat, level base that stays that way: a level concrete pad, or compacted crushed gravel in a timber frame topped with level patio slabs. A deck works only if a structural engineer confirms it can carry the full weight. Grass, soil, sand or slabs laid on soil aren't suitable.

How do you get the hot tub into my backyard?

We move it on its side on a spa dolly, so the path needs to be as wide as the tub is deep and as tall as its longest side, plus a little room for the dolly. Our pre-delivery guide lists the exact path size for every model. If walls or slopes block the way, a crane can lift it over.

Owning a hot tub

How much maintenance does an AquaSolus hot tub need?

Very little. The double ozonator does most of the work, so the only sanitizer you add is 1 teaspoon of chlorine granules after each soak. Rinse the filter once a week, check calcium once a month, and change the water every 3 to 4 months. A few minutes a week.

Should I use chlorine or bromine?

Chlorine. We don't recommend bromine in our tubs. With the double ozonator doing so much, a teaspoon of chlorine after each soak is all your AquaSolus needs.

How do I look after hot tub water with Lower Mainland soft water?

Our water is naturally soft and acidic, so on each fresh fill raise total alkalinity first, then set pH, then add calcium hardness increaser, then chlorine. Balanced water is gentle on your skin and protects the heater and jets.

Is a hot tub expensive to run in our winters?

An AquaSolus is built to run lean. AQ10 insulation keeps the pumps, heater and every line inside the insulated envelope, so heat stays in the water and the pumps' own warmth goes back to work. Keep the cover on and locked whenever you're not soaking.

What happens if I need service?

Every AquaZen customer gets an account with their agreement, delivery photos, serial number, care guides and a service request button that comes straight to us. We recommend a service visit at least once a year.

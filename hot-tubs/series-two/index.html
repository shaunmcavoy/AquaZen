Why it's different.

A massage that pulls the tension out.

It's not the number of jets, it's how the water reaches you. Most hot tubs use skinny plumbing, like a thumb over a garden hose, and the sharp spray can feel itchy. Aquasolus uses wide plumbing that delivers four to five times more water to every jet, so a big, warm volume of water works over more of your back and draws the tension out.

Water this clean takes more than ozone.

A typical hot tub takes about 45 minutes to move all its water through the filter and ozone. With four to five times more water flowing through the jets, an Aquasolus does it in under 10. Every series has a double ozonator: ozone held under water in a mixing chamber for at least 45 seconds, then a UV stage. Cleaner water, fewer chemicals.

Easy on the planet. And your hydro bill.

Full AQ10 insulation keeps the heat the pumps make inside and puts it back into the water, instead of losing it to the air. Less wasted heat, less electricity.

Build quality, second to none.

A 304 stainless steel frame that won't rot or rust, on a structured ABS base pan that seals and protects the bottom of the tub. Built for the longest life you can get.

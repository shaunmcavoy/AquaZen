A mix of small, medium, large and extra large jets for every part of your back.

Why it's different.

Water this clean takes more than ozone.

Every hot tub has an ozonator. Ours holds the ozone under water in a mixing chamber for at least 45 seconds so it actually works on the water, then sends it past a UV light. The cleanest water possible in a hot tub.

Easy on the planet. And your hydro bill.

Full AQ10 insulation keeps the heat the pumps make inside and puts it back into the water, instead of losing it to the air. Less wasted heat, less electricity.

Build quality, second to none.

A 304 stainless steel frame that won't rot or rust, on a structured ABS base pan that seals and protects the bottom of the tub. Built for the longest life you can get.

Shaun's honest take

Plug and play is simple, with one trade-off. On a regular outlet the heater and the jets share the power, so on a chilly night the water takes longer to warm back up. If you'll soak every night all year, have a look at Series Two. If you want the easiest start, this is it.

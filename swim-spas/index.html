Why it's different.

Clean water, simply.

Easy on the planet. And your hydro bill.

Full AQ10 insulation keeps the heat the pumps make inside and puts it back into the water, instead of losing it to the air. Less wasted heat, less electricity.

Shaun's honest take

A swim spa is a bigger project than a hot tub. Full of water they weigh between 5,048 and 13,180 kg, so you need a proper base, a 240V supply and room to get it into the yard. We'll talk through the base, the power and the access with you before you order, so there are no surprises on delivery day.

A closer look.

Built in

Two ways to swim

River jets give a smooth, adjustable current. Professional models use a 9-speed hydraulic turbine for a wider, deeper flow.

Hydrotherapy seats

When the workout is done, sit back under the jets and let your muscles recover.

Gecko K1001 controls

One panel for the current, the jets, the lights and the temperature.

Light and sound

Colour lighting and Bluetooth audio come standard on every swim spa.

Built to last

A 304 stainless steel frame, and AQ10 insulation that keeps the heat in the water instead of the air.

Why it's different.

Clean water, simply.

Ozone water care comes standard, and soft Lower Mainland water keeps the routine short. We show you exactly how before delivery.

Easy on the planet. And your hydro bill.

Full AQ10 insulation keeps the heat the pumps make inside and puts it back into the water, instead of losing it to the air. Less wasted heat, less electricity.

Built to carry the weight.

A 304 stainless steel frame that won't rot or rust, made to hold thousands of litres for years.

Every AquaSolus swim spa comes with

Award winning

The AquaSolus Swim Spa Series is a WhatSpa? Best Buy award winner.

Three ways to set it up

Professional: a 9-speed turbine for a smooth, deep current, from an easy recovery swim up to a 0:56 per 100 m pace. Standard: three river jets for a broad current. Dual Zone: a swim lane and a separate hot tub, each at its own temperature.

Six-colour LED lighting

Lights above and below the waterline, with six shades from Halo to Ember.

Bluetooth sound with a subwoofer

Stereo speakers and a subwoofer come standard. Drift and Solara add two transducer speakers you feel through the water.

Two shell finishes

Aristech acrylic in Sterling Silver or Odyssey, in a Luna Grey composite cabinet with a 304 stainless steel frame inside.

Designed in England

AquaSolus is a Superior Wellness brand, designed and engineered in England.

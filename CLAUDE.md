# AquaZen website

Static site for AquaZen (legal name Aqua Zen Wellness Consulting, operating as AquaZen), Shaun McAvoy's hot tub, swim spa and mini pool store at 7049 Russell Ave, Burnaby, BC. Live at https://aquazen.ca via GitHub Pages from `main` (root). Phone 236-304-4424, email shaun@aquazen.ca.

- Products: AquaSolus (Canadian range at https://aquasolus.com/ca/). No O-Care (Lower Mainland water is soft). Photos in `assets/img/` came from AquaSolus.
- Design: Apple-style product page. Black and #f5f5f7 sections, system font (SF Pro on Apple, Inter fallback), the logo's sunset gradient used only on "Find your Zen" headlines. Scroll effects in `assets/js/site.js` respect reduced motion.
- Pages: `index.html` (home), `find-your-zen/` (the main CTA and lead capture). Styles in `assets/css/site.css`.
- Hot tub pages are generated: never hand-edit `hot-tubs/`. Edit `src/tubs.json` (specs from aquasolus.com/ca, our own taglines and fit notes) or `build.py`, run `python3 build.py`, commit both. Series pages: hero, short series intro and stats, then every model as a clickable card (no spinning scene), then features, build, compare table and honest take. Model photos live in `assets/img/models/`. Vacation Series has no Canadian model pages yet, so it links to Find your Zen.
- Model pages link to `/find-your-zen/?model=<slug>`; finder.js passes it to the lead as `interested_in`.
- Brand language: Why, How, What (SCP / Start With Why). The Why is a place of peace at home; hot tub time is "Zen Time"; main CTA is "Find your Zen". Do not use "Stop going. Start being." Founder: over twenty years in the industry, last three consulting for hot tub stores. Never mention Bishop's.
- Tagline: "Your Zen is calling." (footer, title). The why is about the customer: choosing a better evening, disconnecting on purpose. All selling backs it with evidence that the tub delivers the four expectations: luxury, healthy low-maintenance water, eco-friendly and energy efficient, a massage that releases tension. AquaZen exists to exceed what people expect owning a hot tub to be.
- How we deliver Zen chapters: massage, water flow, double ozonator, energy (56-frame pumps, pressure in the lines, corners others cut), insulation (full foam leaves the pumps, heater, control box and the lines around them bare, so heat escapes at the weakest point and the tub keeps running in the cold; AQ10 keeps 100% of the water insulated 100% of the time; then an AQ10 or full foam table). Avoid third-party statistics.
- Spelling: AquaSolus (capital S) everywhere in copy.
- Pumps: the How we deliver Zen page and homepage pitch the 240V customer: say "we use 56-frame pumps, not 48-frame" without qualifying it. Do not mention 120V there. 120V tubs do not need 56-frame pumps and get their own pitch later.
- Gap story: stores hold back on equipment to protect chemical and service revenue; AquaZen sells the right equipment from day one because the best experience keeps you busy.
- Copy rules: no em dashes, Canadian spelling, Shaun's warm plain voice, no invented prices, statistics, reviews or customer stories. Every customer message explains why.

- Pricing (staff portal Products tab, admins only; schema in `supabase/migrations/002_pricing.sql`): `product_lines` (manufacturer, line, pad per unit, show_price), `product_costs` (true cost, msrp, sale_price = Zen price, line_id), `addons` (cost, pad, sale_price, offered). Sales cost = true cost + pad. Salesperson GP = selling price - sales cost; commission = `commission_pct` (25) of GP, paid when the deal is paid in full; price floor `min_margin_pct` (40) is on TRUE cost: lowest price = ceil(true cost / 0.6), a hard rule for hot tubs, swim spas and mini pools only (not add-ons). The floor is hidden: `price_floor()` returns the lowest price only when the offered price is under it, and the `sales_deal_before` trigger blocks non-admins saving below it. The pad is the owner's money: it lowers the salesperson's GP only. Admin profit before commission = price - true cost; after commission = price - true cost - commission. Financials never counts the pad as a cost. No commission when the deal's salesperson is an admin or unset (`004_no_commission_for_admins.sql`). Salespeople never read true cost or pad: they use `sales_catalogue()` / `sales_addons()` / `pricing_settings()`. Settings change only through `pricing_settings_put()` (admin).
- Deal builder (`supabase/migrations/003_deal_builder.sql`): sale form has Add-ons (from `sales_addons()`, Delivery mirrors `delivery_fee`), MSRP/Zen price from `sales_catalogue()`, live GP and commission. `sales.extras` jsonb + `extras_total` + `msrp` (customer-visible). While a sale is draft, triggers recompute tax/total, write `sale_deal` (sales cost, GP, commission; salesperson + admins) and `sale_costs` (true cost, pad, extras_cost, delivery_cost, commission; admins). Once out of draft nothing recomputes, so numbers lock at signing. Never put cost, GP or commission on `sales` (customers can read their row).
- Add-ons on a deal can be at regular price, discounted, or Included (price 0); `sales.extras` items carry `list` (the add-on's regular price) so the agreement shows "Included (a $X value)" or "was $X". Delivery is an add-on in extras now; `delivery_fee` is legacy (older sales move over on their next save).
- Savings breakdown (`AZ.savingsHtml` in app.js, used by the customer view and the signing page; PDF has its own row): MSRP, regular Zen price (`sales.zen_price`, snapshotted from product_costs.sale_price while draft), then any extra discount and add-ons included free shown as bright gradient "bonus" rows, Your price, and You save off MSRP. Extra discounts must look like an over-and-above deal.

- Pricing (staff portal Products tab, admins only; schema in `supabase/migrations/002_pricing.sql`): `product_lines` (manufacturer, line, pad per unit, show_price), `product_costs` (true cost, msrp, sale_price = Zen price, line_id), `addons` (cost, pad, sale_price, offered). Sales cost = true cost + pad. Salesperson GP = selling price - sales cost; commission = `commission_pct` (25) of GP, paid when the deal is paid in full; price floor `min_margin_pct` (40) is on TRUE cost: lowest price = ceil(true cost / 0.6), a hard rule for hot tubs, swim spas and mini pools only (not add-ons). The floor is hidden: `price_floor()` returns the lowest price only when the offered price is under it, and the `sales_deal_before` trigger blocks non-admins saving below it. The pad is the owner's money: it lowers the salesperson's GP only. Admin profit before commission = price - true cost; after commission = price - true cost - commission. Financials never counts the pad as a cost. No commission when the deal's salesperson is an admin or unset (`004_no_commission_for_admins.sql`). Salespeople never read true cost or pad: they use `sales_catalogue()` / `sales_addons()` / `pricing_settings()`. Settings change only through `pricing_settings_put()` (admin).
- Deal builder (`supabase/migrations/003_deal_builder.sql`): sale form has Add-ons (from `sales_addons()`, Delivery mirrors `delivery_fee`), MSRP/Zen price from `sales_catalogue()`, live GP and commission. `sales.extras` jsonb + `extras_total` + `msrp` (customer-visible). While a sale is draft, triggers recompute tax/total, write `sale_deal` (sales cost, GP, commission; salesperson + admins) and `sale_costs` (true cost, pad, extras_cost, delivery_cost, commission; admins).

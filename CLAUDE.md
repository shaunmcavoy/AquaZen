# AquaZen website

Static site for AquaZen (legal name Aqua Zen Wellness Consulting, operating as AquaZen), Shaun McAvoy's hot tub, swim spa and mini pool store at 7049 Russell Ave, Burnaby, BC. Live at https://aquazen.ca via GitHub Pages from `main` (root). Phone 236-304-4424, email shaun@aquazen.ca.

- Products: Aquasolus (Canadian range at https://aquasolus.com/ca/). No O-Care (Lower Mainland water is soft). Photos in `assets/img/` came from Aquasolus.
- Design: Apple-style product page. Black and #f5f5f7 sections, system font (SF Pro on Apple, Inter fallback), the logo's sunset gradient used only on "Find your Zen" headlines. Scroll effects in `assets/js/site.js` respect reduced motion.
- Pages: `index.html` (home), `find-your-zen/` (the main CTA and lead capture). Styles in `assets/css/site.css`.
- Hot tub pages are generated: never hand-edit `hot-tubs/`. Edit `src/tubs.json` (specs from aquasolus.com/ca, our own taglines and fit notes) or `build.py`, run `python3 build.py`, commit both. Series pages have the scroll scene (`assets/js/tubs.js`): overhead photos tilt and turn as you scroll. Model photos live in `assets/img/models/`. Vacation Series has no Canadian model pages yet, so it links to Find your Zen.
- Model pages link to `/find-your-zen/?model=<slug>`; finder.js passes it to the lead as `interested_in`.
- Brand language: Why, How, What (SCP / Start With Why). The Why is a place of peace at home; hot tub time is "Zen Time"; main CTA is "Find your Zen". Do not use "Stop going. Start being." Founder: over twenty years in the industry, last three consulting for hot tub stores. Never mention Bishop's.
- Tagline: "Your Zen is calling." (footer, title). The why is about the customer: choosing a better evening, disconnecting on purpose. All selling backs it with evidence that the tub delivers the four expectations: luxury, healthy low-maintenance water, eco-friendly and energy efficient, a massage that releases tension. AquaZen exists to exceed what people expect owning a hot tub to be.
- Pumps: 240V hot tubs use 56-frame pumps (pitch is why we use the bigger pump, not a claim). 120V tubs do not need them; 120V and 240V are different customers and different pitches, so never say "every tub" has 56-frame pumps.
- Gap story: stores hold back on equipment to protect chemical and service revenue; AquaZen sells the right equipment from day one because the best experience keeps you busy.
- Copy rules: no em dashes, Canadian spelling, Shaun's warm plain voice, no invented prices, statistics, reviews or customer stories. Every customer message explains why.
- Find your Zen recommendations are computed in `assets/js/finder.js` and sent to Shaun only, never shown to the customer.
- Backend: Supabase project `zfqzajshbjfmflbttabq` (AquaZen, in the Sales Closers Playbook org). Table `leads` (RLS on, no public policies). Edge function `lead` (`supabase/functions/lead/index.ts`, verify_jwt false) saves the lead and emails via Resend from shaun@mail.aquazen.ca (domain verified in the SCP Resend account). Secrets: Resend key (saved in the dashboard as RESENC_API_KEY; the function also reads RESEND_API_KEY), optional LEAD_TO. Never put keys in the repo or in chat.
- DNS is at name.com. Google Workspace MX records serve shaun@aquazen.ca; keep them. Keep `CNAME`.
- Back office (ported from North Grenville): `login/`, `staff/`, `sign/`, `portal/` are generated by `build_app()` in build.py from `src/app/*.html`, with `assets/css/app.css` and `assets/js/{app,login,staff,sign,portal}.js` (global `AZ`). Server: `supabase/functions/portal/index.ts` + `_shared/{common,pdf,qb}.ts`; the deployed `portal` function is a shim importing index.ts from a pinned commit SHA on GitHub (verify_jwt false), so redeploy the shim after pushing a change. Schema in `supabase/migrations/001_back_office.sql`. Admin is shaun@aquazen.ca (`admin_emails`).
- Sales: BC tax is GST 5% + PST 7% (`tax_rate` 0.12). Make is Aquasolus. No saunas. Payment is paid in full or deposit now with the balance later; the balance invoice is sent from the sale (route `qb-balance-invoice`), and `qb-sync` (pg_cron every 15 min) marks deposit and balance invoices paid.
- Delivery window: at signing the customer picks "asap" (under two weeks) or a two-week window starting on a Monday, stored in `sales.delivery_window` (YYYY-MM-DD of the first day). Staff book the exact day (`delivery_date`) from the sale.

Sandbox Company CA 8cc0 is for testing: set secret QB_ENV=sandbox with the Development keys; remove QB_ENV for production. QB_CLIENT_ID and QB_CLIENT_SECRET live only in Supabase function secrets. The qb-sync cron call needs timeout_milliseconds 60000 (cold start). Redirect URI https://zfqzajshbjfmflbttabq.supabase.co/functions/v1/portal/qb-callback.

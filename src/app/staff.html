    How pricing works

    Each line has a pad: an amount added to our true cost before the salesperson's GP is worked out. It stays with the business (it covers exchange, freight, operations, marketing and profit). A salesperson only ever sees the sales cost: our true cost plus the pad. Their GP is the selling price minus the sales cost, and they earn the commission below on that GP once the deal is paid in full. True costs and pads are only visible to admins.

    Price list

    Type a true cost, MSRP or Zen price right in the table and it saves on its own. What the salesperson sees is their GP and commission. Profit before commission is the price less true cost: what you keep when you sell it yourself. The pad stays with you; it only lowers the salesperson's GP. Profit after commission is what's left once a salesperson is paid.

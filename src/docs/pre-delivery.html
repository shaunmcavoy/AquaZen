Why we ask

A hot tub is heavy, it needs its own power, and it has to travel from the street to your backyard. When the base, the power and the path are ready, delivery takes a few hours and you’re soaking soon after. When one of them isn’t, everybody waits.

Plan

Before we book your delivery

When you signed, you told us when you’d like your tub: as soon as possible, or a two-week window that suits you. As that time comes up, we’ll call to book the exact day. Before we do, these five things need to be done.

  1. Choose the spot

    Somewhere safe, easy to reach from the house, with room to service the tub on every side. See page 4.

  2. Build a solid, level base

    Flat, level and strong enough to carry a full tub with people in it, for the life of the tub. See page 4.

  3. Have your power installed

    A licensed electrician runs a dedicated circuit and a disconnect, under a permit. See page 5.

  4. Measure the path

    From the street to the spot: widths, heights, steps and tight corners. See page 6.

  5. Send us photos

    Your driveway, side yard, backyard and the final spot, or one short walk-through video. See page 7.

Top tip

Call your electrician this week.

Good electricians book up fast in spring and summer, and the permit adds a little time. Getting on their calendar early is the easiest way to keep your delivery window.

Your balance

If you paid a deposit, your balance is due before delivery. You can pay it right from your customer portal. That way delivery day is only about getting you into the water.

Pre-Delivery Guide03

Prepare

Your spot and your base

Picture yourself walking out in January, in the rain, in a robe. A short, safe path from the door is what turns a hot tub you own into one you use every night.

Choosing the spot

  • Never under or near overhead power lines.
  • Keep it out of roof drip lines, where rain, snow and ice slide off onto the cover.
  • Leave about 50 cm (20 in) behind the tub if you’re adding a cover lifter, so the cover can fold down.
  • Keep metal railings, fences and furniture at least 1.5 m (5 ft) away, or have your electrician bond them.
  • Near a slope or low spot? Make sure rainwater drains away from the base, not under it.
  • A sheltered spot, privacy screen or gazebo keeps the wind off the cover, and that saves energy all winter.
Service access

Leave at least 60 cm (24 in) clear on every side, especially the side with the equipment. Everything in your AquaSolus sits inside the insulated cabinet and is easy to reach through the side panels, as long as we can get to them. If we can’t, the tub has to be drained and moved before any service, at your cost.

Building the base

Your base must be solid, flat and level, and stay that way. Your AquaSolus has a sealed ABS base underneath, so it’s protected from damp ground, but it still needs even support across the whole footprint.

Electrical in BC

Your tub’s power must be fully installed before delivery day. Every tub needs its own dedicated circuit from your main panel, never shared with anything else.

Your tubWhat it needs

Brook, Zephyr, Cypress and Cove		A dedicated 15 A 120V GFCI-protected outdoor receptacle with a weatherproof cover, never an extension cord. Or a 40 A 240V line, so the heater and jets run at full power together.
Series Two and Three
Every other hot tub		A dedicated 60 A 240V line.
Swim spasVaries by model. See the swim spa table.

For 240V tubs

  1. Hire a licensed electrical contractor. They take out the permit and arrange the inspection.
  2. They run a dedicated circuit to a GFCI disconnect within sight of the tub, at least 1.5 m (5 ft) from its edge. AquaSolus suggests about 2 m (6.5 ft) where you can.
  3. They leave enough cable to reach the tub’s equipment. Ask us which corner it’s in for your model. More is better than not enough.
  4. They make the final connection to the tub. Book them for delivery day so we can fill it and get it heating before we leave. We’ll help you line up the timing.
Important All electrical work, including the GFCI disconnect, must be done by a licensed electrician. If it isn’t finished, we can still set your tub in place, but it can’t be started.
Permits in BC

In most of BC, your electrician takes out the permit with Technical Safety BC. Some cities issue their own, including Burnaby, Vancouver, Surrey, the City and District of North Vancouver, West Vancouver, Maple Ridge and Victoria. Your electrician will know which applies to you.

Access

Getting it into your yard

We move your tub on its side on a spa dolly. So the path needs to be as wide as the tub is deep, plus room for our hands, and as tall as its longest side, plus the dolly.

{{access_table}}

Worked out from each model’s AquaSolus size. Not sure about a spot? Send us a photo with a tape measure in it.

Walk the path and check for

  • Gates, fence posts, downspouts, window ledges and meters that narrow the way.
  • Low branches, eaves, gutters, wires and pergolas.
  • Tight corners, especially around the side of the house.
  • Steps, slopes and retaining walls. Tell us how many and how high.
  • Gas, hydro and water meters, A/C units and vents.
  • BBQs, play equipment, patio furniture and planters to move.
  • Room on the street or driveway for our truck.
Cranes

When the path won’t work

If walls, steep slopes or a full fence block the way, a crane or boom truck can lift your tub over. It’s arranged and paid for directly with a crane company. We never lift a tub over a wall by hand. Swim spas almost always come in by crane because of their size and weight.

Please note Once it’s set down, it’s there to stay. With a crane, make sure your base is ready and the tub goes straight to its final spot while the crane is on site.

Pre-Delivery Guide07

Enjoy

Delivery day

We’ll confirm the day with you ahead of time and call when our team is on the way. Here’s how it goes.

  1. Unload

    Our team stands your tub on its side on a spa dolly.

  2. Move and place

    We bring it along your path and set it on your base, in its final spot.

  3. Level and check

    We make sure it sits flat and level, with the side panels easy to reach.

  4. Record it

    We note your serial number and take a few photos. They go straight into your customer portal, so a warranty claim never means hunting for paperwork.

  5. Fill and start

    Once your electrician has connected it, we fill it from your garden hose, start it up and show you the controls. It’s usually warm by the next evening.

  6. Welcome to Zen Time

    You’ll get an email from us with everything you need for your first soak. Ask us anything. No question is too small.

How AquaSolus delivers it

Built for the things that matter.

Every drop that comes out of a jet goes back through the filter, the double ozonator and the heater before it comes back to you. Here’s what’s underneath.

10 min

Water turned over, fast

High-flow circulation moves all of the water through the filter and ozone in under 10 minutes. A typical hot tub takes about 45.

45 s

A double ozonator, then UV

Ozone is held under water in a mixing chamber for at least 45 seconds, breaking down bacteria, yeast, mould, algae and biofilm, then the water passes a UV stage.

56

56-frame pumps, not 48-frame

Bigger pumps and wide plumbing keep the pressure in the lines, for a deep, soft massage instead of a sharp, itchy spray.

100%

AQ10 insulation

The pumps, heater and every line sit inside the insulated envelope. Full foam leaves them bare. AQ10 keeps 100% of the water insulated, 100% of the time.

304

Stainless steel frame

A hand-welded 304 stainless steel frame that won’t rot or rust, on a sealed ABS base that keeps out cold, damp ground and critters.

You

Shaped around you

Sculpted seats and headrests, jets placed for specific muscle groups, and a diverter that sends the flow to the seats that need it most.

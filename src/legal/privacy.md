## Who we are

AquaZen is the operating name of Aqua Zen Wellness Consulting, a hot tub, swim spa and mini pool store at 7049 Russell Ave, Burnaby, BC, run by Shaun McAvoy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through our website, our customer portal and our staff portal, why we collect it, and how we protect it. Questions about your information go to Shaun at shaun@aquazen.ca or 236-304-4424.

We follow Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), British Columbia's Personal Information Protection Act, and Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL).

## What we collect and why

- **When you fill out Find your Zen:** your answers (who's soaking, how often, where it goes and what matters to you), your name, email, phone and area, and the best time to reach you. We use it only to call you back with honest advice, and we save it in our customer records so Shaun has your answers in front of him when you talk.
- **When you buy from us:** your name, address, city and postal code, phone, email, how you heard about us, what you bought, the price, deposit and balance, the delivery window you choose, and your signature. We use it to prepare and keep your sales agreement, order and deliver your hot tub, register your warranty, and look after you as a customer.
- **When you sign an agreement online:** the date and time you signed, the name you typed, your drawn signature, and the internet address and browser you signed from. We keep this as proof of the agreement, the same way we'd keep a paper copy.
- **When you use the customer portal:** your sign-in email and the details of your purchase, so you can see your agreement, your delivery and where things stand.
- **When you message us on Facebook or Instagram:** your messages and the name Meta shares with us. Noah, our AI on social media, answers with the help of Anthropic's Claude, and a person on our team can read the conversation and take over.
- **When you chat with Noah on our website:** your messages, anything you choose to tell him (like your name, phone number or email), the page you were on and, if you sent Find your Zen, your answers and results. Your browser keeps the chat (and a random ID for it) so it carries across our pages. Noah answers with the help of Anthropic's Claude, and a person on our team can read the conversation.
- **When you open a link Noah sent you:** that link is private to your conversation. While you're on the presentation or Find your Zen, the page tells us how far you've got and when you leave, so Noah can follow up with a helpful question. We don't track anything else you do online.
- **Website visits:** our site uses the Meta Pixel to count visits and Find your Zen forms, so we can tell which ads are worth running. You can limit this in your Facebook ad settings or by blocking cookies.
- **Staff:** name, email and phone for the people who sell for us, plus the sales they made.

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for anything you wouldn't expect from your hot tub store.

## Who helps us run this

We use a small number of trusted service providers. Each only gets what it needs to do its job:

- **Supabase** stores our customer and sales records in Canada (Montreal).
- **Resend** sends our emails. It may process your email address and the email content in the United States.
- **QuickBooks Online (Intuit)** handles our invoices and payments. It receives your name, email, phone, address and the invoice amount when we bill you.
- **GitHub Pages** hosts the public website, which doesn't store your personal information.
- **Meta (Facebook and Instagram)** carries our messages with you and receives website visit information from the Meta Pixel.
- **Anthropic** provides the AI that helps Noah write his replies. It receives the conversation, in the United States.

Some of these providers may store or process information outside Canada, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

## Payments

When you pay an invoice online, you pay through QuickBooks. We never see or store your card or bank account numbers.

## How long we keep it

We keep sales agreements and purchase records for as long as needed for warranty, service and tax purposes, generally at least seven years. If you fill out Find your Zen and don't buy, you can ask us to delete your answers at any time.

## Your choices and rights

- **Ask to see** the personal information we hold about you, or **ask us to correct** it, by emailing shaun@aquazen.ca.
- **Ask us to delete** information we no longer need. Some records, such as signed agreements and tax records, we're required to keep.
- **Stop emails** from us at any time by replying to one and telling us.

## How we protect it

Access to customer records is limited to Shaun and the staff who need it, and each salesperson sees only their own customers. Signed agreements are stored privately and shared only through short-lived links. Our QuickBooks connection is only used to create customers and invoices and to see when they're paid.

The right equipment.
From day one.

Some stores hold back on equipment so they can sell you more chemicals and service calls later. We think that's backwards. Give people the best experience possible and they'll keep you busy for years. That's why we chose Aquasolus.

Water this clean takes more than ozone.

Every hot tub has an ozonator. Saying so is like saying a car has an engine. Ours goes further. The ozone is held under water in a mixing chamber for at least 45 seconds, so it actually works on the water instead of bubbling away. Then the water flows past a UV light before it comes back to you. The result is the cleanest water possible in a hot tub, with less to think about.

Easy on the planet. And your hydro bill.

Full AQ10 insulation wraps the shell, cabinet and base. The heat the pumps make while they run is kept in and put back into the water instead of being lost to the air. Less wasted heat means less electricity. It's the most energy-efficient spa we've found in the Lower Mainland.

Build quality, second to none.

A 304 stainless steel frame that won't rot or rust, set on a structured ABS base pan that seals and protects the bottom of the tub. Built for the longest life you can get from a hot tub.

Plug in and soak.

Select Series One models run on a standard 120V outlet, so you can start without a new electrical line.

Made by hand.

Each Aquasolus tub is handcrafted, with a design inspired by water, simplicity and balance.

Some stores hold back on equipment so they can sell you more chemicals and service calls later. We think that’s backwards. The right equipment from day one means more nights in the water and less time looking after it. Here’s what that looks like inside an Aquasolus.

Water this clean takes more than ozone.

Every hot tub has an ozonator. Saying so is like saying a car has an engine. What matters is how much water passes through it. On average, a hot tub takes about 45 minutes to move all of its water through the filter and ozone. That slow cycle is why so many owners rely on so many chemicals. With four to five times more water coming out of every Aquasolus jet, all of it has to come back through the system. The filter pulls out what we bring in. Then it goes through a double ozonator, standard in every Aquasolus series: first the ozone is held under water in a mixing chamber for at least 45 seconds to break down bacteria, yeast, mould, algae and biofilm, then the water passes a second, UV stage before it's heated and sent back to you. The whole tub is cleaned in under 10 minutes, more than four times faster. Cleaner water, fewer chemicals, less to think about.

A massage that pulls the tension out.

A great massage isn't about how many jets a tub has. It's about how the water reaches you. Most hot tubs push water through skinny plumbing, like putting your thumb over the end of a garden hose. The spray comes out sharp, and many people feel it as an itchy sting instead of relief. Aquasolus does the opposite. Wide plumbing delivers four to five times more water to every jet, so a big, warm volume of water works over a larger area of your back. It gets the blood moving through tight muscles and draws the tension out from deep inside. Think of a big rain showerhead next to a skinny one. Hydrotherapy works the same way.

Easy on the planet. And your hydro bill.

Full AQ10 insulation wraps the shell, cabinet and base. The heat the pumps make while they run is kept in and put back into the water instead of being lost to the air. Less wasted heat means less electricity. It's the most energy-efficient spa we've found in the Lower Mainland.

Bigger pumps that work less.

Aquasolus pairs that wide plumbing with large 56-frame pumps. The bigger wet end moves more water without fighting back pressure in the lines. A pump that isn't straining draws less electricity and lasts longer.

Build quality, second to none.

Each tub is handcrafted on a 304 stainless steel frame that won't rot or rust, set on a structured ABS base pan that seals and protects the bottom of the tub. Built for the longest life you can get from a hot tub.

Select Series One models run on a standard 120V outlet, so you can start without a new electrical line.
